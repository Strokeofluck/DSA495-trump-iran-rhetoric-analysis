    How were the means calculated?

    For each frame and event date, average the scores of the unique posts in that date/window. Then take the unweighted mean of those event-date means. A busy event date gets the same weight as a quiet date. Missing groups are omitted, never replaced by zero. A date can contribute to one or two windows without having posts in the remaining windows.

    This reproduces the original notebook’s available-date estimator. It has no non-event control group or causal design. We do not treat overlapping event dates as independent observations or report significance tests.

    What exactly does the model receive?

    The original notebook sends saved content_text to the Transformers zero-shot pipeline with multi_label=True, truncation=True, and hypothesis template This text expresses {}. It scores each unique post once. The prepared text is not stemmed, stopword-filtered, or lowercased by an additional preprocessing step in that notebook. No training or fine-tuning is performed here.

    • Threat: military threats, escalation, retaliation, ultimatums, warnings, strikes, or destruction directed at Iran.
    • Victory: claims of victory, military success, Iranian weakness, defeat, surrender, decimation, or successful U.S. operations.
    • Diplomacy: diplomacy, negotiations, talks, deals, conditions, agreements, or communication with Iran or allied governments.
    • Ceasefire / Ending: a ceasefire, stopping military operations, ending the conflict, pausing attacks, or declaring the fighting essentially finished.
    • Blame Allies: blaming or criticizing NATO, European allies, or other countries for not helping enough or being unhelpful.
    • Media Criticism: criticizing, blaming, or attacking the mainstream news media, journalists, or press coverage as biased, fake, or dishonest.
    Which data version is this?

    This page uses only the two committed CSVs in this project repository: 87 unique posts, 129 post/date pairs, 26 event dates. The README describes a later portfolio revision with different counts. Those data are not mixed into this poster. The workbook supplies event records and same-day fatality estimates; rhetorical means come from the committed event-window CSV and saved scores. Workbook release labels are Jan-Jun 2026 v26.01.26.06 and July 2026 v26.0.7. The source viewer flags inconsistent bounds on multi-day record 625182; that cumulative record is excluded from the severity plots.

    These files are derived inputs. Rebuilding the poster from them is reproducible; reconstructing the exact original upstream collection and reproducing model inference still requires pinned source and software versions.
